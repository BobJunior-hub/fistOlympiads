Frequently asked

The honest answers.

Still curious? Email hello@halftankapp.com — a real person, usually within a day.

How much does the halftank fuel card cost?+
Setup is free. There are no monthly fees and no hidden charges — you pay only for the fuel you buy. The card pays for itself the first time you use it.
How much can I really save per gallon?+
Up to $1 per gallon at participating stations. Actual savings depend on where you fuel, when, and how much. Drivers on the platform average a meaningful double-digit reduction on monthly fuel spend.
Where can I use the card?+
At 3,000+ participating truck stops across the United States. The app shows you which ones, and the Smart Route Fuel Finder highlights the cheapest stops along your destination.
Do I need the physical card?+
No. You can pay without the physical card directly from the app — handy if you lost it, forgot it, or just prefer mobile. Online PIN setup and security controls keep it locked down.
What if I run a fleet?+
Small Fleet covers up to 5 vehicles with shared reporting and discounts. Large Fleet adds custom volume rates, a credit line up to $5,000 per card, prepayment options, and a dedicated account manager.
Can I export my fuel records?+
Yes — PDF and Excel reports any time, broken down by card, driver, or vehicle. Great for expensing, accounting, and tax season.
How do I get help if something goes wrong?+
24/7 support — a real person, not a chatbot. Hit "Request a call" anywhere on the site and a manager will be in touch within 5 minutes during business hours.
